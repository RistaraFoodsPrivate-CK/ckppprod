Prod NameIngredientUOMNet Qty Used / UnitRM Qty Used / UnitWastage %Type
Net Qty Used is the usable / cleaned quantity of the ingredient needed per 1 unit of the Prod Name's production. RM Qty Used is the raw, unprocessed quantity that must be pulled and preprocessed to yield that net quantity. RM Qty Used is what drives the preprocessing requirement — because every recipe is a 1-kg recipe, it is multiplied directly by the Daily Production Qty, not divided.

Type = CS and Type = PP mark the Ingredient as a sub-recipe. Define the CS/PP as its own Prod Name (for example CS - Mint Chutney) with quantities per 1 kg or its stated output UOM, then use that CS/PP in the CP product. A CS is maintained as its own recipe. Example: CS - Boiled Sona Masoori Rice has a 1 kg output basis and contains Sona masoori rice 0.286 kg and RO Water 0.857 ltr. If a CP recipe uses this CS, the system scales these CS ingredients according to the CP recipe quantity. Type = OTHER is for ingredients (like sugar, salt, oil) that should never appear on the preprocessing plan.
